## 3. Capture and numerical criteria

The five positive cases capture every resource to 64×166 R32FLOAT atlases. The
three negative cases capture their eight headers plus plan to 64×1 atlases; their
unchanged physical source fields are not duplicated in the log. The complete
dump is **53,312 float32 words in 296 bounded log lines**, including guards and
250 once-accepted/attempt counters. Partial final hex rows are length checked.
State becomes `observed` only after logging finishes.

The decoder requires:

- Twenty-five exact source controls, sixty-four exact header controls and five
  exact eligible plans; all three negative plans must have zero dt/active flag.
- Sixty logical/bank bit-identical pairs, twenty end-only/full-gather checks,
  intermediate copies, admission, nonnegative mass, zero invalid recipients and
  zero atlas padding.
- The existing per-axis/cumulative End/Mean conservation bounds (64/128 ULP).
- All 3,640 map endpoints inside the existing s134 conditional expression-graph
  enclosure, evaluated from the captured inputs and actual plan dt. The model's
  finite/normal/reciprocal assumptions remain unchanged; it is not a CSP compiler
  conformance proof or general runtime numerical admission.
- Full-time cases retain the original strict same-backend s137 controls. Local
  WARP also requires all 130 consumer fields to match frozen s138 bits. Actual
  partial-time WARP pointwise comparisons are recorded separately as backend
  diagnostics; partial actual eligibility uses the stated invariant/enclosure
  gates. No prior stage criterion is changed or overwritten.

No result grants full physical closure, the full legacy consumer gate, host
queue commit authority, target timing or production adoption.
